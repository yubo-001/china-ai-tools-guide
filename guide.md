## 1. Overview

China's AI ecosystem has evolved from a "copycat" narrative to a genuine innovation powerhouse. As of mid-2026, China accounts for:

- **36%** of global AI research papers
- **42%** of the world's AI patent filings
- **15+** major LLM platforms with public APIs
- **3 of the top 10** open-source model contributors

The landscape is fragmented, fast-moving, and often opaque to international observers. This guide cuts through the noise.

### Key Trends

- **Open-weight dominance**: DeepSeek, Qwen, and ChatGLM all release MIT/Apache-licensed models
- **Video generation leapfrog**: Kling (Kuaishou) is publicly available while Sora remains in waitlist
- **Cost war**: Chinese API pricing is 5-20x cheaper than US equivalents
- **Vertical integration**: Major platforms (ByteDance, Alibaba, Baidu) offer end-to-end AI stacks
